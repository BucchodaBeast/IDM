## Intakes and start dates
The 2026/27 brochure covers the January and June intake for higher education studies at the Eswatini campus. The three-year part-time CIPS Diploma Level 4 is available in the June intake only.

## Bachelor of Arts in Human Resource Management (BA HRM)
NQF Level 7. Part time, 2 years. Entry requirement: a Diploma in HR from a recognised institution. Career opportunities: Corporate Affairs Manager, Talent Manager, Administration Officer. Human Resource Management students also pay an IPM subscription fee of E650.

## Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSc IT)
NQF Level 7. Full time 3.5 to 4 years; part time 4.5 to 5 years. Entry requirements, any one of these. EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level: 6 passes with 5 at C grade or better, including English Language and Mathematics, plus 2 required subjects at C or better from Physical Science, Information Technology, Physics, Design and Technology. NSC, ISC or Matriculation with a Bachelors admission recommendation, including a pass in Mathematics or any 2 of Physical Science, Information Technology, Computer Applications Technology, Computer Studies or Design and Technology. Or a Diploma in a relevant qualification. Careers: Information Technology Manager, IT Project Manager, Systems Analyst, System Developer, Web Developer, Database Administrator, IT Network Administrator, End User Support.

## Bachelor of Management in Education and School Leadership (BMESL)
NQF Level 7. Full time 4 years; part time 5 years. Entry requirements, any one of these. EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level: 6 passes with 5 at C grade or better, including English Language. National Senior Certificate, International Secondary Certificate or Matriculation with a Bachelors level recommendation and a minimum of 6 passes including English Primary Language. Or a Diploma in a relevant qualification. Careers: school leader, administrator, manager or Head of Department in schools, technical and vocational centres, early childhood development centres or any other educational institution or training facility.

## Bachelor in Occupational Health and Safety (BOHS)
NQF Level 7. Full time 3.5 to 4 years; part time 4.5 to 5 years. Entry requirements, any one of these. EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level: 6 passes with 5 at C grade or better, including English Language, plus 2 required subjects at C or better from Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physical Science, Combined Science or Additional Combined Science. NSC, ISC or Matriculation with a Bachelors level recommendation, including a pass in English Language and 2 required subjects from Physical Science, Life Sciences or Biology, Geography, Mathematics. Or a Diploma in a relevant qualification. Careers: OSH management specialists in manufacturing plants, construction sites, healthcare facilities and government agencies.

## Bachelor of Community Development (BCD)
NQF Level 7. Full time 3.5 to 4 years; part time 4.5 to 5 years. Entry requirements, any one of these. EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level: 6 passes with 5 at C grade or better, including English Language. NSC, ISC or Matriculation with a Bachelors admission recommendation including a pass in English Primary Language. Or a Diploma in a relevant qualification. Career: professional community development practitioner.

## Diploma in Information Technology
NQF Level 5. Full time 2 years; part time 3 years. Entry requirement: EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level with at least 5 passes including 3 credits in Mathematics or Physics and 2 other credits. Careers: IT Officer, Network Administrator, Software Designer, Systems Developer.

## Diploma in Human Resource Management
NQF Level 5. Full time 2 years; part time 3 years. Entry requirement: EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level with at least 5 passes including 3 credits. Careers: Human Resource Officer, Administrator, Administration Officer, Compensation Officer, Assistant Training Officer, Learning and Development Officer. HR students also pay an IPM subscription fee of E650.

## Diploma in Occupational Health and Safety (D.OHS)
NQF Level 5. Full time 2 years; part time 3 years. Entry requirements, any one of these. EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level: 5 passes with 3 at C grade or better and a minimum of grade D in English Language and any other subject, with 2 required subjects at C or better from Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physical Science, Combined Science or Additional Combined Science. NSC, ISC or Matriculation with a Diploma level recommendation including a pass in English Primary Language and 2 subjects from Physical Science, Life Sciences or Biology, Geography, Mathematics. Or a Certificate in a related qualification. Careers: Occupational Safety and Health Officer, Industrial Hygienist, Workplace Wellness Coordinator.

## Certificate in Freight Forwarding and Custom Compliance
NQF Level 4. Part time, 18 months. Entry requirement: SGCE, IGCSE or Matric with 4 credits including English. Careers: Clearing Agent, Logistics and Distribution Officer.

## Beginners Tax Certificate
Part time, 12 weeks. No prior knowledge of tax is needed; relevant working experience helps. Leads to tax understanding and compliance.

## CISCO certificates: Cyber Security and CCNA 1, 2 and 3
CISCO Certificate in Cyber Security: part time, 6 months; entry is 5 passes plus relevant working experience; careers include Information Security Officer, Cyber Security Analyst, Certified Ethical Hacker. CCNA 1 Introduction to Networks: part time, 12 weeks; entry is an IT related course or relevant working experience. CCNA 2 Switching, Routing and Wireless Essentials: 12 weeks, part time; entry is the CISCO CCNA 1 certificate. CCNA 3 Enterprise Networking, Security and Automation: 12 weeks, part time; entry is the CISCO CCNA 2 certificate. CCNA careers: helpdesk, technician, network administrator, network support technician.

## CIPS procurement and supply programmes
CIPS Certificate Level 2 (18 credits): 6 months full time or 1 year part time; entry is 5 passes. CIPS Advanced Certificate Level 3 (30 credits): 1 year; entry is the CIPS Level 2 Certificate. CIPS Diploma Level 4 (60 credits): 1 year; entry is the CIPS Advanced Certificate, or at least 2 years relevant work experience, or at least 2 Advanced Level passes or international equivalent, or a relevant qualification. CIPS Advanced Diploma Level 5 (60 credits): 1 year; entry is the CIPS Level 4 Diploma. CIPS Professional Diploma Level 6 (60 credits): 1 year; entry is the CIPS Level 5 Advanced Diploma. CIPS Diploma Level 4 three-year part-time option, June intake only: entry is 5 credits at EGCSE, IGCSE or GCE O-Level. Careers: tender-awarding personnel, purchasing officer, logistics officer, store keeper; Levels 5 and 6 lead to Strategic Sourcing Manager, Supply Management Analyst, Logistics Analyst, Supply Chain Manager.

## Other fees: application, supplementary, invigilation, IPM
Application fee: E200. Supplementary fee for IDM courses: E500. Computer based invigilation fee: E400. IPM subscription fee: E650, applicable to Human Resource Management students only. CIPS exam and registration fees are set by CIPS and payable in British Pounds Sterling. Prescribed textbooks are billed separately from tuition.

## How to apply and documents required
Application forms are submitted with certified academic certificates, a certified copy of the national ID, and the application fee of E200. Students must pay a prescribed deposit before academic registration. The deposit is waived for students who can show evidence of financial aid or a scholarship.

## Payment methods and terms and conditions
Pay tuition into IDM's Standard Bank account 911 000 3233 998, FNB account 631 043 753 73, or MTN Mobile Money number 7809 0080. Send proof of payment to payments@idmbls.ac.sz or 7682 6651. Students may not attend classes without paying the required deposit. Tuition fees are subject to an annual increase communicated before the new academic year. Refunds follow the Institute's Refunds Policy and start with the application for refund form.

## SNAT members group discount
SNAT members get a group discount: 10% for 15 to 19 participants, 15% for 20 to 29 participants, 20% for 30 or more participants.
